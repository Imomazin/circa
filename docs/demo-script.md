# Demo script (6–8 minutes)

A guided walkthrough of Circa's commercial-intelligence story. The same steps are available in-app under **Demo**, each linking straight to the right screen. Sample business: **Caledon Furniture Works** (furniture refurbishment + take-back).

> Opening line: *"Circa answers one question — does this circular business opportunity make commercial sense? Everything you'll see is synthetic data, and the scores are prototype decision-support, not validated measures."*

| # | Screen | Do | Say | Viewer should notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Overview** (`/`) | Land on the dashboard. Change the sector filter. | "This is the portfolio. Viability by sector, capital by circular model, and — importantly — a viability-versus-evidence scatter." | Filters recompute every chart; the scatter's top-left flags strong cases with thin evidence. |
| 2 | **Businesses** → Caledon | Open Caledon Furniture Works. | "A furniture refurbishment and take-back opportunity — strong demand, but supplier-concentration risk." | Five commercial scores across the top; models and stage as badges. |
| 3 | **Assessment** (`/assessments/caledon-furniture-works`) | Scroll the dimension cards. | "Each score decomposes into weighted components with positive and negative drivers." | The score is explained, not asserted. |
| 4 | **Business profile** | Point to Circular opportunity + rationale. | "Here's the opportunity and the commercial rationale in plain English." | Resource dependencies and supplier risks are concrete. |
| 5 | **Scenarios** (`/scenarios/caledon-furniture-works`) | Drag the **Revenue change** or **Material cost change** lever. | "Change one commercial assumption…" | Every output in the comparison table moves instantly. |
| 6 | **Scenarios** | Watch EBITDA, payback, cash requirement; click **Save**. | "…and the financial scenario updates. Saving persists it." | Baseline/circular/upside/downside compared; save confirms. |
| 7 | **Assessment → Viability card** | Expand Commercial Viability. | "Viability blends market pull, margin, capital efficiency, payback and risk." | Component meters and weights are visible. |
| 8 | **Viability drivers** | Read the drivers. | "These drivers explain the number; the amber flags show what evidence is missing." | The 'why' behind the score. |
| 9 | **Resilience card** | Point to supplier diversification. | "Supplier concentration pulls resilience down — a specific, actionable finding." | Resilience is a distinct, meaningful axis. |
| 10 | **Investor readiness** (`/investor-readiness/caledon-furniture-works`) | Show the radar + capital ask. | "How fundable is it today, what's the capital need, and what must happen before funding?" | Evidence gaps and actions before funding. |
| 11 | **Investment case** (`…/case`) | Open, then **Print / PDF**. | "A print-friendly one-pager that pulls the whole story together." | Clean, executive layout; prints cleanly. |
| 12 | **Programme intelligence** (`/programme`) | Zoom out. | "For policy and programme teams — anonymised, aggregate: where the value sits, what capital it needs, and where support helps." | No business-level data; benchmarks and support needs. |

**Close:** *"Deterministic, explainable, database-backed — and it runs with no external AI. All synthetic, all prototype. The Accelerator would take this to real co-design, empirical calibration and live data."*

## What not to claim

- Do not present scores as validated or as official Zero Waste Scotland outputs.
- Do not imply CivTech endorsement or a production deployment.
- Always state that the data is synthetic and the scores are prototype decision-support.

## Reset

If a previous run left edits, use **Demo → Reset demo data** (type `RESET`) to restore the deterministic seed before presenting.
