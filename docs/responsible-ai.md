# Responsible decision support

Circa uses deterministic rules. No model API is called and no AI-generated narrative is presented as evidence.

The interface exposes component scores, weights, confidence and evidence gaps. Humans choose assumptions, review the output and decide what action to take. No investment, lending or eligibility decision is automated.

Synthetic records cannot establish accuracy or fairness. Methodology development must examine differences in sector economics, company size, geography, data availability and language or digital-access needs. Weak documentation must not be assumed to mean a weak business.

Manual review occurs through visible scenario assumptions and assessment stage changes, recorded in the audit trail. Arbitrary score overrides are not implemented. Future overrides should require a named reviewer, reason and preserved prior result.

Future narrative providers must retain source provenance, distinguish inference from observation and require human acceptance. Empirical calibration, uncertainty assessment, bias analysis and user research remain future validation work.
